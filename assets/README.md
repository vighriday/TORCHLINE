# Asset provenance

The shipped local fonts and owner-supplied Claude Design adaptations are recorded in [design provenance](design-provenance.md). Ship original or explicitly licensed artwork, fonts, sound and animation. Links in private inspiration notes do not establish permission to use their contents.

Record each shipped asset here or in a dedicated register with filename, creator/source URL, license, required attribution, modifications and proof of permission where relevant. Prefer original SVG/CSS ship systems and puzzle visuals for the first implementation. Do not add unlicensed background music to the demonstration.
