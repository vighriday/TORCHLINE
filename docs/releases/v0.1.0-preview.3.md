# v0.1.0-preview.3

Two games, two communities, one Ark. This development preview makes TORCHLINE's relay story playable end to end without a Twitch account. It also makes every saved decision checkable and adds a one-command local start.

## What changed

- **Judge Arena relay rehearsal.** Eleven steps move one Ark between two practice communities, using the same endpoints as the dashboard.
  - Alpha's LIVEWIRE lava jump fails (hull −6).
  - A labelled simulated raid hands the ship to the receiver.
  - The receiver's aerial goal repairs the same hull (+3).
  - Alpha's decision becomes the receiver's chapter-two crisis.
- **Inheritance dossier.** The receiving stream sees it on the dashboard and in the read-only OBS view:
  - who handed the Ark on;
  - every captain commitment and the community that made it;
  - what other games did to the hull;
  - which systems were lost;
  - each pending debt, linked to the decision that caused it.
- **Chronicle.** A lineage graph of communities × chapters with saved cause links and custody markers. It comes with a season record, an on-stream season finale, and revocable read-only public links.
- **What if they had chosen differently?** Replays every option of a saved decision once, from the exact state before it. It shows the match only when the recomputed choice reproduces the saved record. Later chapters are never simulated.
- **Verify this record.** Replays the whole saved log through its pinned rules and compares SHA-256 digests of the replayed and the saved ship.
- **LIVEWIRE presets.** Each preset has its own illustrated scene and game-styled HUD: a voxel lava jump, a car-soccer aerial and a souls-style boss duel. The streamer names the game they actually play.
- **Platform.**
  - Shared channel projections, bounded retention and attributed custody arrivals.
  - Per-client request budgets and feed recovery within seconds.
  - A season finale and many interface fixes.
- **One-command preview.** `docker compose up --build` starts saved practice with LIVEWIRE on, and CI starts the stack on every push.

## Upgrading

An existing database needs migrations 012–014 before this build starts. Run `pnpm db:migrate`, or `pnpm db:migrate:live` for the local live environment. Take a backup first.

## Evidence and limits

- **Automated checks:**
  - Vitest: 164 tests in 43 files against real PostgreSQL.
  - Playwright: 25 browser journeys, including the full two-game relay.
  - Release-container and compose smoke tests.
  - The Verify workflow on `main`.
- **Live Twitch:** on 30 October 2026, three genuine viewer chat commands arrived as signed EventSub notifications and were saved as gameplay events on a live Ark.
- **Still open:**
  - a recorded overlay update from live chat;
  - an attested live LIVEWIRE result;
  - a native raid between two consenting broadcasters;
  - bot replies that viewers can see;
  - permanent hosting.
- The Judge Arena raid proof is simulated and labelled as such.
