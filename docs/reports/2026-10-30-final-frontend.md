## Remaining acceptance

- Owner visual/usability review and real mobile Twitch video readability. Responsive browser checks do not measure a compressed stream on a phone.
- Sustained device/OBS performance, source scene switching and actual outgoing broadcast. Manual frame/DPR bounds are not minimum FPS guarantees.
- Richer physical mesh deformation beyond navigation loss and the seven-system permanent-mark/record mapping. Do not describe every system as a separate structural damage simulation.
- Genuine signed viewer gameplay → saved consequence → visible overlay, then native raid plus receiving consent. Historical query chat and provider-accepted invisible replies do not close these gates.
- Permanent deployment, deployed edge/SSE behavior, off-host backup durability and remaining audit A07/A11, broader load/retention and v3 balance work.

The local final-design preview is usable while these gates remain open. The existing public release is not silently relabeled as this candidate. Keep the dedicated bot's role and the native custody requirements unchanged.
